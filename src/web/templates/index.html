What works

This reads tab notation off the video frames — it does not listen to the audio. It needs a tutorial that shows the tab on screen, the kind produced by a tab player. Most ukulele tutorials that display tabs are of this kind.

A tutorial where someone only plays and talks, with no tab on screen, will not produce a useful sheet. Processing takes a few minutes, and the result page reports how much of the piece was actually captured.
